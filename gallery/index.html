Particle tracing in a Taylor tokamak equilibrium with ITER geometry, simulated using the Lorentz miniapp in MFEM. The simulation captures both passing and trapped particles, with the latter exhibiting characteristic banana orbits. Image courtesy of Rushan Zhang, as part of the 2026 MFEM Visualization Contest. 🖼️ 🎬
